Minnesota Cardboard is an app for anyone with a big collection of playing
cards: collectors, players and sellers who sort, sell, find the best deals,
share and keep track of their cards. It's in development for Windows, macOS and
Linux.

## What it will do

- **Add cards three ways:** a document scanner, a photo, or typing the card in.
- **Digitally sort for you:** every card you add is sorted the way you set it
  up, with optional SKUs for sellers.
- **Know your inventory:** what you have, and what has already sold.
- **See prices** from several markets, for one card, a deck or your whole
  collection.
- **Search everything:** find cards by anything about them.
- **Build and share decks** from your collection, and share decks or whole
  collections with a link.
- **Sell anywhere:** list cards on Minnesota Cardboard, eBay, TCGplayer,
  Facebook Marketplace and more. The app finds where each card gets the best
  deal for you and your buyers, lists it there, and shows detailed analytics
  about your sales: what sold, where, for how much, how fast, how much buyers
  had to pay on fees versus the listed card price (pre-tax), and more.

## Card games

Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, One Piece Card Game and Yu-Gi-Oh! are planned.

## The app and the website

This site will do most of what the app does, right in your browser, so most
people won't need to install anything. The desktop app is for power users who
want everything Minnesota Cardboard can do.

## Where it stands

It's early. Currently, the app opens a slightly styled window with a navbar and
the core of the internal API is mostly complete. The database has just started
to be modeled and constructed. And the website is awaiting custom functionality
the application/libraries are supposed to provide, so the next step is to
complete the internal API, start really working on the database and public
API/libraries, and creating a nice looking GUI.
