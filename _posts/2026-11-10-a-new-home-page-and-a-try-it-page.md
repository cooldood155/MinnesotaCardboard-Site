The site got a big update:

- **A new home page:** it finally explains what mncb actually does, simply, and
  without huge (text book looking) blocks of text. Scan a stack, take a
  picture, search for and pick your cards from the list of cards (Magic: The
  Gathering, Pokémon, One Piece Card Game, Yu-Gi-Oh!, or all of them), or
  import the ones you already have (images or from another site/app). mncb
  handles the rest (the way *you* set it up).
- **Words, given definitions:** new to trading cards (or to mncb)? There's a
  box on the [home page]({{ "/" | relative_url }}) that explains every word the
  site uses, like binder, deck, box, finish, printing, etc.
- **Setups:** see the built-in setups mncb will come with, and what each one
  does. Able to be created and customized with the paid version.
- **Selling (whats possible):** the home page lists every site mncb plans to
  sell on, and what each one lets apps do **right now**. Some don't let apps in
  yet. You'll know exactly what mncb can (and can't) do on each one.
- **A Try it page:** the newest test build is always on the
  [Try it page]({{ "/try/" | relative_url }}), with the download for your
  computer first. No more digging through the news posts to find it.
- **Red!** It's my favorite color. The whole site is red now (in light and dark
  mode).
- **Every screen:** phones, tablets, Chromebooks, big monitors and TVs now
  properly size and display the sites elements.
- **Report a problem:** there's a link at the bottom of every page now.

I also fixed a bunch of typos, and added a note to
[Introducing Minnesota Cardboard]({{ "/" | relative_url }}) since some of the
plan changed.

Found something wrong, or have an idea? [Tell me here]({{ site.report_url }})!
