A round of updates to this site:

- **Light and dark mode:** the button at the top of every page switches
  between System, Light and Dark. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
- **Your time zone, everywhere:** dates and times on every page are shown in
  your own time zone.
- **Author pages:** every post shows who wrote it, and each author has a page
  with their posts. See [all authors]({{ "/authors/" | relative_url }}).
- **An archive:** older posts can be archived. They leave the news feed but
  keep their own pages, listed on the
  [archive page]({{ "/news/archive/" | relative_url }}).
- **App and website news, separately:** the
  [news page]({{ "/news/" | relative_url }}) now has App and Website tabs,
  and each has its own feed.

To follow updates in a feed reader:

- Everything: [mncardboard.com/feed.xml]({{ "/feed.xml" | relative_url }})
- App only: [mncardboard.com/feed/app.xml]({{ "/feed/app.xml" | relative_url }})
- Website only: [mncardboard.com/feed/website.xml]({{ "/feed/website.xml" | relative_url }})

If you already follow `/feed.xml`, you don't need to change anything.
