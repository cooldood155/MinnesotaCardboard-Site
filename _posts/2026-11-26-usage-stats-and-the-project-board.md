The site now counts how it's used, and shows what I'm working on:

- **Usage stats, without cookies:** the site counts which pages get read,
  how people find it, and what's slow or broken. That's how I'll know what
  to fix first. No cookies, no ads, nothing from anyone else's site, and
  your IP address is never kept. Everything that's counted (and why) is on
  the [Usage stats page]({{ "/help/usage-stats/" | relative_url }}).
- **Don't Count My Visits:** a button at the bottom of every page. If your
  browser sends *Global Privacy Control* or *Do Not Track*, nothing's
  counted to begin with.
- **The app asks first:** starting with the next test build, the app only
  sends its usage stats if you say yes, and *Settings > Show What's Sent*
  shows exactly what goes.
- **The project board:** every bug and idea, and what I'm working on right
  now, is on [mncb's project board]({{ site.board_url }}). It's linked at
  the bottom of every page, on the [roadmap]({{ "/roadmap/" | relative_url }}),
  the help pages, and the Try it and working build pages.
- **Smaller fixes:** tables are only as wide as what's in them, and the game
  names under the logos on the home page are easy to read in dark mode.

Found something wrong, or have an idea? [Tell me here]({{ site.report_url }})!
