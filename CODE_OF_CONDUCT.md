# Code of Conduct

mncb is for everyone who loves trading cards, and so is everywhere people
talk about it: the issues, pull requests, emails, the site and anywhere else
someone speaks for mncb.

## Be good to people

- Be kind, even when you disagree. Say what's wrong with the idea, never
  what's wrong with the person.
- Assume people mean well. Most bad reports are just rushed ones.
- Help new people. A lot of mncb's users have never used anything like it,
  and some have never filed a bug before.
- Keep it about cards and the app.

## Not okay

- Insults, slurs, put-downs, or going after someone for who they are.
- Harassing anyone, in public or in private, or keeping at someone who asked
  you to stop.
- Sharing someone's private information (where they live, their email, their
  collection) without asking.
- Sexual stuff, threats, or anything meant to scare someone.
- Scams: fake trades, fake sales, pretending to be mncb or me.
- Spam and ads.

## If it happens

Email [evanreumann@mncardboard.com](mailto:evanreumann@mncardboard.com) with
**Conduct** at the start of the subject: what happened, where, and links or
screenshots if you have them. It stays between you and me.

What can happen, from least to most: a private warning, a comment or post
taken down, a timeout from the repos, or being removed for good. Anything
that breaks the law goes to whoever handles that.

The person reported gets a chance to tell their side, unless that would put
someone at risk.
