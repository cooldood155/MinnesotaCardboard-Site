{%- comment -%} A for one date. Jekyll writes it in UTC (timezone: UTC in _config.yml) and labels it "UTC"; assets/js/local-dates.js rewrites it in the reader's time zone. - date_only=true, or a date with no time (00:00 UTC) will only show the date. - short=true: "Oct 13, 2026", for lists. Example use: {% include local-time.html date=post.date date_only=post.date_only %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign time_of_day = include.date | date: "%H:%M:%S" -%} {%- if include.date_only or time_of_day == "00:00:00" -%} {%- if include.short -%}{{ include.date | date: "%b %-d, %Y" }}{%- else -%}{{ include.date | date: "%A, %B %-d, %Y" }}{%- endif -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign iso_date = include.date | date_to_xmlschema -%} {%- if include.short -%}{{ include.date | date: "%b %-d, %Y" }}{%- else -%}{{ include.date | date: "%A, %B %-d, %Y, %-I:%M %p" }} UTC{%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
