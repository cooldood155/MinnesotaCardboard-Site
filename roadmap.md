# Roadmap

Where mncb is going, in the order I'm planning it. No dates: a version is out
when it's ready (dates are set as goals internally but may not be met so they
are kept private). What's in each version exactly is its
[milestone](https://github.com/cooldood155/MinnesotaCardboard-Site/milestones),
and every bug and idea is an
[issue](https://github.com/cooldood155/MinnesotaCardboard-Site/issues) you
can follow (or add to). What I'm working on right now is on
[the project board]({{ site.board_url }}).

## Now: 0.1.x

Fixes for what testers find in 0.1.2. Nothing new unless it's small and
asked for.

## Next: 0.2.0

The bugs testers hit, and the app looking like an app:

- **Imports that never double your cards**, with a look at what's coming in
  before it does
- **Backups that *(optionally)* happen by themselves**
- **Keeping the log after a crash**, so a report can say what happened
- **An installer**, a better **Help** menu, **keyboard shortcuts** for
  everything common, and cleaner **spacing and icons**
- **How mncb makes money** (and what it never does), written down

## After that

- **Scanning a whole stack** through a document scanner, as fast as it
  scans
- **Searching by anything:** color, type, rarity, mana value, price, art
- **Fast with a big collection** (tens of thousands of cards)
- **A welcome screen** for your first two minutes
- **Screen readers and keyboard focus** everywhere

## On the way to 1.0

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| your pictures | scans and photos straightened, cropped and kept as each card's picture |
| sellers | SKUs in your own format, labels for boxes and binders |
| players | decks with counts, legality and what's missing, exported for deck sites |
| selling everywhere | list once on every store that lets apps in, taken down everywhere when it sells |
| the phones | the Android and iPhone apps working, then in Google Play and the App Store |
| trust | signed downloads (no more warnings), an update check, a privacy page |

## Later

- **This site** doing most of what the app does, on anything with a screen
- **Accounts and sync** between the app and the site
- **A public API**
- **Sharing** a collection or deck with a link
- **Collection value over time**, **graded cards**, **a for-trade list**
- **Real or fake** from a picture
- **More games**

## What won't change

- You never type cards in.
- Your collection is a file you own: backed up, exported, taken anywhere.
- Nothing about you is sent anywhere without asking.
- mncb only uses a site the way that site allows.
