# Getting Started

Your first ten minutes with MinnesotaCardboard (mncb). Everything else is in
[Using mncb](/help/using/).

## 1. Get it

Go to [mncardboard.com/try](https://mncardboard.com/try/). It picks the
download for your computer (Windows, Mac or Linux). Unpack it and open
**mncb**: nothing gets installed. The page says how to open it on each
system, and what to do about the warning.

> **Note:** The warning is because the app isn't signed yet. On Windows it's *More
> info > Run anyway*. On a Mac the page says how to let it open.

On a phone: not yet. The Android and iPhone apps don't work yet.

## 2. Get the cards to pick from

mncb needs to know every card of a game before you can add yours (it never
asks you to type a card in).

1. Click **Cards & prices** on the left (under *More*).
2. Click **Get Them** next to your game.

It downloads in the background. Keep going while it does.

## 3. Pick how your cards get sorted (optional)

*Settings > Where new cards go > Start from a setup*. Not sure? **Keep it
simple** puts everything in one box. You can change it any time.

## 4. Add your first cards

Pick one of these:

| Way | How |
| :-- | :-- |
| **pick it** | **Add cards** (Ctrl+N): type part of the name, pick the printing, **Add to Collection** |
| **scan it** | **Scan** (Ctrl+Shift+N): **Learn Them** once, then **Open Pictures...** or **Watch a Folder...** for your scanner |
| **bring it in** | **Import & export** (Ctrl+I): another app's CSV (Moxfield, ManaBox, Deckbox, TCGplayer, ...) |

## 5. See what you have

**Collection** shows every card, what it's worth today, and where it is.
Click a card to see all its prices on the right.

## 6. Back it up

**Import & export > Back Up...**. Do it every so often, and before trying
anything big.

> **Careful:** A test build keeps its own collection, and stops working 30 days after
> it's built. Back up anything you want to keep.
